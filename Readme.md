# 🌍 Nigeria Malaria Analysis (2000–2024)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Nigeria's malaria trends over a 24-year period (2000–2024) using World Health Organization (WHO) malaria datasets.

The objective was to explore how malaria prevention efforts, disease burden, and incidence have changed over time, and to generate data-driven insights using healthcare analytics.

---

## Business / Healthcare Questions

This analysis aimed to answer:

- How has Insecticide-Treated Net (ITN) coverage changed from 2000–2024?
- Has increased prevention coverage translated into reduced malaria burden?
- How has malaria incidence changed over time?
- What patterns can be observed in Nigeria's malaria response?

---

## Dataset

**Source:** World Health Organization (WHO) Global Health Observatory (GHO)

Indicators analyzed:

- Insecticide-Treated Net (ITN) coverage
- Estimated malaria cases
- Malaria incidence per 1,000 population at risk
- Uncertainty intervals for malaria estimates

Time period:

2000–2024

---

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

The datasets were prepared before visualization by:

- Removing unnecessary columns
- Handling missing values
- Standardizing indicators
- Transforming data into analysis-ready formats
- Creating measures for trend analysis

---

## Key Findings

### Prevention Coverage

- ITN coverage increased steadily between 2000 and 2024, showing expansion of malaria prevention efforts.

### Malaria Burden

- Despite improvements in prevention coverage, malaria burden remains significant and varies over time.

### Malaria Incidence

- Overall malaria incidence showed a declining trend, although fluctuations occurred across different years.

### Healthcare Implication

The findings highlight that malaria control requires multiple approaches, including prevention tools, vaccination strategies, improved surveillance, and stronger health systems.
